Key takeaway: Productivity Center processes and stores its data locally in browser extension storage. It does not send your data to a developer-controlled server, use analytics, or sell your information.

Productivity Center is a browser extension for time management, task lists, website & keyword blocking, and local activity summaries. This Privacy Policy explains what the extension accesses and how that information is handled.

1 What information does the extension use?

To provide its features, the extension uses the following information locally in your browser:

  • Website domains and time: When an accessible active tab has a web address, the extension records its hostname and increments an activity counter.
  • Website and keyword blocking: The extension checks against added websites and keywords and blocks a match.
  • Tasks: Task text and completion state entered in the Quick Tasks feature.
  • Settings: Timer state and durations, focus-mode state, and your keywords and blocked-site list.
The extension does NOT collect: passwords, credit card numbers, personal messages, search history, or any content from the websites you visit.

2 Where is your information stored?

The information above is stored exclusively in the browser's local extension storage. The extension does not use a remote database or cloud sync service.

  • Your data is stored only on your own computer.
  • The developer does not have access to your data.
  • It is not transmitted to the developer or shared with third parties.

3 How do we use your data?

Data is used solely to power the extension's features:

  • Productivity stats: to show you a report of how much time you spent on specific sites.
  • Website & keyword blocker: The extension does not request access to any website at install time. When you add a website to block, it asks for access to that specific site (and its subdomains) so it can enforce your block list. For keyword blocking, the extension needs broader access because keyword matching can apply to any URL. If you decline, the item is not added. The access is used only to enforce the block list you configure while Focus Mode is on; the extension does not read page contents.
  • Pomodoro timer: to track your work and break intervals.

4 Limited Use

The use of information received by Productivity Center will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data accessed by the extension is used only to provide the user-facing features described in this policy — Pomodoro timing, task management, website and keyword blocking, and local activity summaries.

5 Third-party sharing

No data is shared with anyone. No third-party advertisers, analytics companies, or marketing firms have access to your data.

6 Your rights

You have full control over your data:

  • You can view your stats and settings at any time within the extension.
  • You can delete all locally stored extension data by clicking "Clear all data" in the Settings. This also resets the timer and removes active custom blocking rules.
  • Removing the extension removes its locally stored data according to the browser's extension-storage behavior.

7 Security

Since your data is stored locally on your device, it is not exposed to external servers. Standard browser security applies. Keeping your browser and operating system up to date is recommended.

8 Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the "Last updated" date on this page will be updated. Continued use of the extension constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

9 Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how the extension handles data, you can reach the developer at LaisurRahman@proton.me.
